About

Nouar AlDahoul is a Research Scientist Associate at NYUAD, specializing in artificial intelligence, environmental modeling, healthcare applications, and computational social science. She received her Ph.D. in Engineering from the International Islamic University Malaysia in 2019. She previously served as a postdoctoral associate at NYUAD, where she led experimental studies on gender and racial bias in generative AI, safety in large language models (LLMs), content moderation in media, misinformation detection, and political ideological leanings in LLMs. Her work bridges technical innovation with ethical inquiry, advancing fairness, safety, and interpretability in AI systems.

In parallel with her research, Nouar has worked extensively as a trainer with "Tertiary Courses Malaysia" and "Iverson Malaysia," delivering over 60 workshops to individuals, academic groups, and corporate teams. Her sessions cover a wide range of topics, including AI, machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, the Internet of Things (IoT), and LLMs. She emphasizes hands-on learning, conceptual clarity, and real-world application, empowering diverse audiences to engage meaningfully with emerging technologies.

She has also participated in international conference challenges such as ICIP2020 and ArabicNLP2025, securing first place in both—demonstrating her ability to translate cutting-edge research into competitive, high-impact solutions.
